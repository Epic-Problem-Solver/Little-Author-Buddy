Meet your writing buddy

    This little companion lives on your desktop and keeps you company while you write. Every word you finish helps your buddy's own story. When you take a break, Buddy naps, wanders, and sometimes peeks out from behind a window.

    Only word counts are used. What you type is never recorded, and nothing leaves your computer.

    You can rename Buddy later. The gear on the desk opens Settings.

    The four colours are examples. Any lighter colour works, and the gear can change it later.

    Shading and hands follow the colour you pick. A body dark enough to flip that shading lighter cannot be chosen. The outline, desk, and writing tools stay their default colours.

    What are you writing? Your buddy starts a short story of 3,000 words with you.

    A word counts when Space, Enter, or Tab follows a letter or a digit.
