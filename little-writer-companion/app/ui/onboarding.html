Meet your writing buddy

This little companion lives on your desktop and keeps you company while you write. Every word you type helps your buddy finish its own story; when you take a break it naps, wanders around your screen, and sometimes peeks out from behind your windows.

Only keystroke counts are used. What you type is never recorded, and nothing leaves your computer.
